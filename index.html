About

I'm a first-year Information Science student at the University of Maryland, College Park, taking the Data Science cognate. Before this I did the IB diploma at the International Community School of Abidjan, with Computer Science at Higher Level. That course is where I got used to reading an error message instead of panicking at it.

Last summer I worked at CDCA in Abidjan on their weekly financial reporting. It was the same few formatting steps on the same spreadsheets every week, so I wrote Python scripts to do them instead. A job that took about two hours ended up taking fifteen minutes, and the output stopped picking up the small typos you get from doing it by hand.

This semester I'm taking STAT100 and INST201. The plan for the rest of the year is a Python and pandas project on a public dataset, and whatever I finish goes on the projects page.
